Study AI Privacy Policy

Effective 16 September 2026

Study AI is a free Android learning app operated by COLBRYAN NIG LTD. Study AI is for ages 13 and older. Most learning data stays on the learner's device.

Information stored on the device

Study AI stores profile choices, selected subjects, study plans, lesson progress, quiz results, mock-exam history and advertising frequency counters locally on the Android device. The app asks for a broad age group for app access and appropriate advertising protections; it does not ask for a date of birth.

This local information remains until the learner resets the profile and progress, clears the app's storage, or removes the app.

Advertising

Study AI uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform to fund free access and manage privacy choices. Google and its approved advertising partners may process device identifiers, approximate location derived from an IP address, consent choices, ad impressions and ad interactions to deliver, limit and measure advertisements.

Teen learners receive G-rated, non-personalized advertising requests with under-age-of-consent treatment. In version 1.16.0, advertising is not initialized before the age check or for blocked under-13 users. Pilot builds use official test ads; these still connect to Google and earn no revenue. Adult learners receive Google's consent choices where required by law. Study AI does not send a learner's name, subjects, answers, learning progress or exact age to advertisers.

Google describes its data practices in Google's Privacy Policy and its partner-sites information.

Online learning services

When the learner checks for reviewed question updates, the app contacts the Study AI content service. When the learner deliberately opens Online Challenge, the app requests general-knowledge questions from Open Trivia DB. These services receive ordinary network information such as the device's IP address and request details. Online Challenge results remain separate from curriculum progress.

AI Tutor pilot

AI Tutor is restricted to approved adult pilot testers declaring they are 18+. Teen learners retain lessons, practice and mock exams. When an adult taps Ask tutor, questions and selected study context are sent through Cloudflare to OpenAI. The adult age category is sent to the Cloudflare gateway to enforce the pilot restriction. Do not enter names, contact details or other personal information in questions. AI can make mistakes; check important explanations with a teacher.

The gateway stores hashed access identifiers, request fingerprints and temporary answer receipts for quotas and retries. Answers may be retained for up to 48 hours. From version 1.17.0, six-character codes expire after 24 hours or one use. An access credential is encrypted on the Android device for up to seven days; a pending code is also encrypted to support activation retries. The gateway stores credential hashes, code HMACs, expiry times and organiser-assigned tester nicknames or IDs. Organisers can revoke access. Hourly cleanup removes expired codes and sessions, and removes inactive tester records after 30 days without a live code or session. Hashed IP-based activation counters remain for about 24 hours, plus the cleanup interval; raw IP addresses are not saved in those counters. OpenAI's provider retention rules also apply; store:false is not Zero Data Retention. See OpenAI's privacy policy and Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Sharing and sale of information

COLBRYAN NIG LTD does not sell learners' personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers described above, when needed to operate the app, comply with law, protect users, or enforce applicable terms.

Children's privacy

Study AI is intended for ages 13+. From version 1.16.1, users confirm they are at least 13 and choose either 13–17 or 18+ before using the app, including after an upgrade. Previously recorded under-13 profiles remain blocked. This is self-declared age, not verified identity. The first teen pilot is school-supervised in Nigeria. School supervision does not by itself establish parental consent; the school and organiser must review participation arrangements before inviting students. Teen AI access remains paused pending additional safeguards and consent arrangements.

Choices and deletion

Learners can change their age group from Profile, use the Privacy and advertising screen to manage available Google privacy choices, and erase the locally stored profile and progress with Reset profile and progress. The separate age-access record in version 1.16.0 remains after a profile reset. The encrypted pilot credential is separate from profile and progress reset. Forget saved access in AI Tutor removes it; switching to a teen age group also removes it. Device settings can clear all app storage, and uninstalling removes the local credential. Contact support about an incorrect blocked age choice; do not select an inaccurate age to bypass restrictions.

Security and changes

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information handled by the app. No system is completely secure. We may update this policy when Study AI's features, providers or legal requirements change; the effective date above will identify the latest version.
